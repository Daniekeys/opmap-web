# Phase 2 - Lead finder

Read: Core pipelines > 2, Lead sourcing, Data accuracy, Location. Image: `reference/diagrams/location-ladder-and-tile-search.png`.

Build:
- Campaign screen: business type, target kind (Haiku router, user can override), locations from `geo_areas` at any level.
- Seed `geo_areas` with Nigeria's 36 states, the FCT and their main towns. GeoProvider geocodes anything else and caches the boundary.
- Tile search: boundary to a grid of tiles about 4 km across, keeping only tiles that overlap the boundary circle or polygon (square corners dropped), Places Text Search restricted to each tile's rectangle, page to 60, split any tile that returns 60 into 4 (stop at about 250 m). AI neighbourhood queries run on top as extras.
- `search_coverage` written per tile and query; re-runs and expansions skip fresh tiles.
- Field mask limited to the Lead sourcing list (Enterprise tier). Drop non-operational businesses.
- Every Places call goes through checkLimit: hard stop at the per-environment cap (production 50 a day and 650 a month, development 10 a day and 300 a month, since 2026-11-01; env can lower them, never raise them, since 2026-11-03), with "Paused until" and the time the cap lifts shown on the campaign in the workspace's time zone (Lagos time until 2026-11-06; the daily cap lifts at midnight Pacific time, the monthly cap at the start of next month). Nothing resumes it by itself (production too, since 2026-11-01): an owner taps Resume search once the cap has lifted (judged by Postgres's clock).
- Dedupe on place id, normalised domain, E.164 phone, and fuzzy name within 200 m.
- Haiku fit filter on name and category, 20 businesses per call, through the Batch API when not urgent.
- For fit leads with a website: crawl it for emails, phones, `wa.me` links, socials, owner names and schema.org data. Everything lands in `contact_points` with source, confidence and label.
- For fit leads without a website: no search at lead-finding time (spec, Lead sourcing > Local business flow 6). When a user first opens the lead, the deep-dive runs one web search on the lead's phone number and name, and saves a social only if its cited text contains that phone, or names the business and its city. Built in Phase 2 as the `lead_socials` pipeline behind `research.openLead`; the Phase 3 lead page calls it.
- Cost estimate on the campaign confirm button (tiles x queries x pages).
- Added 2026-11-01: leads wanted per campaign (20 to 30, default 20; migration 0028). The search ends as done once that many leads pass the fit check, checked after every results page; an idea that runs out of good fits ends as done with what it found (spec, Location > Leads wanted).
- Added 2026-10-30: per-campaign search settings on the confirm panel (search budget, searches a day up to the shared cap, pages per tile 1 to 3, neighbourhood searches 0 to 3) with a live estimate; Edit campaign (search terms, places, settings) while draft, paused, stopped or failed; Stop search on a running or paused campaign, and Continue search on a stopped one. Owner only (spec, Location > Search settings, edit and stop).
- Added 2026-10-30: the campaign page's progress (spec, Pipelines): the search's five steps from `step_runs` with the design's marks, Claude credit and Google searches against the caps in config, the paused state naming the limit and when it resets, and Stop with a confirm. `GET /campaigns/:id/progress`, read-only, with its leak test. A search paused on a limit scores what it found under `dedupe.sofar` and `fit.sofar` steps; the real dedupe and fit run when it finishes.
- Added 2026-11-06 (Part D, any country): country profiles keyed by ISO code (spec, Location > Beyond Nigeria) for phone parsing, search language, time zone, name words, law, opt-out, notes, channel order, likely WhatsApp and the messaging rule; full profiles for NG, GH, KE and GB, a default profile with a "check local marketing rules" note for every other country. The workspace time zone in `workspaces.settings`, suggested from the Brand DNA, editable on Settings. No migration.
- Added 2026-11-06 (Part D4): a place not in the list is looked up on Google Maps from the campaign form (a `geo_lookup` run, one metered geocode) and cached in `geo_areas` under where it sits: towns under their first-level division, districts under their town, first-level divisions we did not seed as `region` (searched as one area). Fake-provider tests cover Accra, Nairobi, Camden and Manchester, and Accra and Camden campaigns end to end. No migration.
- Added 2026-11-06 (Part D5): "Google Maps has little data for this area" on the confirm panel for China and North Korea (`thinMapsData` in the country profiles), and on a campaign whose finished search found fewer than 3 businesses. No migration.
- Added 2026-11-06 (Part D6): a UK lead's legal form from Companies House in the deep-dive (`deepdive.legal_form`), saved on the business for 90 days; call opener only unless an active company or a member's "I checked: limited company"; TPS/CTPS line on the UK call tab. Migration 0029 (`businesses.legal_form`, `businesses.legal_form_checked_at`, `leads.legal_form_confirmed`).
- Added 2026-11-06 (Part D3): `/campaigns/new` prefills the places from the approved Brand DNA's location facts (`GET /campaigns/suggested-places`, no Google call), with a note naming the fact; a buyer segment's suggested places load as full rows (`GET /geo-areas?ids=`). The user can pick any other place.
- Leads table: name, category, city, rating and reviews, best phone, email and WhatsApp with their labels, fit, and a "Researched / Not yet" status so people know what opening a lead will do. On phones the table becomes stacked cards.

Done when: a campaign for one business type in Ilorin returns at least 50 deduplicated leads without passing the daily cap, and a manual check of 20 rows finds the contact labels honest.
