# Phase 2.5 - Buyer segments

Read: Overview, Core pipelines > 1 and 2, Lead sourcing, Quality guardrails. `00-explee-flow-breakdown.md` step 3 (Define campaigns) for reference only; never copy their UI or copy.

The value is matching, not search: the tool reads the seller's Brand DNA, works out who is most likely to pay for what they sell, and finds them locally. No Google calls in this phase.

Build:
- Brand DNA sources belong to the workspace: a website's domain is held by one workspace (`workspace_domains`), another workspace's crawl of it is refused, and only a version whose source is the workspace's own is ever used or approved. Afriment's version 2 (read from daniekeysstudios.com) is retired.
- Specs in sync: the "Keep the specs in sync" rule in CLAUDE.md (both repos); Overview flow, Core pipelines 1 and 2, the Scope table and this phase updated; decisions log rows.
- Brand DNA without a website: a "describe your business" path, a short text plus an optional Instagram handle. Same Opus extraction to the same Zod schema, same approve-facts flow, same versions. Facts from a description are sourced to that description (its excerpt must be in the text), so they are approved like crawled ones. Instagram pages are never fetched; the handle is kept with the profile, and the user can paste their bio or captions into the description.
- Buyer segments: a versioned prompt with a Zod schema. Input: approved Brand DNA facts only (`getApprovedFacts`). Output: up to 6 segments, each with a name, 2 or 3 Places search terms, why they buy, the pain, what to pitch first, buyer signals as checkable fields (review count, rating, has website, has socials, category), suggested places from `geo_areas` or country names, reachability (`places` or `not_yet`), notes on what to look for that Maps can't check, and a rank with a one-line reason. Prompt v2: `not_yet` only when Google Maps doesn't list these businesses as a business type; a filter Maps can't check (recently funded, hiring, launching) is a note, never a block. Suggested places start where the facts say the seller is based, then add the cities where these buyers cluster when the seller serves the whole country. Ranked by real pain, ability to pay and digital exposure: a business already spending on marketing ranks above one with no online presence, unless the seller's offer is exactly about getting them online.
- Claude Opus 5.5 behind `LlmProvider`, cached per Brand DNA version (and prompt version), cost written to `usage_ledger` through `checkLimit`.
- The campaign screen opens with the segment cards. Pick one, edit it, or type your own. "Not reachable yet" segments are shown but can't start a campaign. The chosen segment is saved on the campaign, with the Brand DNA version it came from.
- Lead ranking: the category rubric must pass before the buyer score counts. Fit check v5: Haiku reports what the name and category say; code settles the name from the campaign's own word lists (`campaigns.name_words`: words for this kind and words for other kinds, made by Haiku from the segment with the segment set (prompt v2: words for this kind must be specific to it, so words that commonly name other businesses too, like "auto", "cleaning", "store", "market", "mini", are left out), or before the campaign's first fit check when the user typed or changed the segment; shown read-only on the campaign screen) (a word for this kind names it, even next to another kind; only a word for another kind, like "Events Palace", names another kind) and applies the rubric. Leads (match or likely) get a buyer score from the segment's buyer signals, stored with its reasons on the fit check; the leads table sorts by it and shows the top reason in plain words ("35 reviews and an active Instagram"), and names the score in words: Strong fit at 0.7 and above, Good fit from 0.5, Fair fit below. A lead below likely scores 0 and sits last, unless promoted by hand: then it keeps its score and shows "Promoted by you". The note on a lead is Haiku's name-and-category sentence plus the listing (reviews, website, socials) built by code from current data when it's read.
- Re-score the existing Ilorin campaign with a bakery segment from Daniekeys' Brand DNA, Haiku only, no Google.
- "Add a fact" on the Brand screen: no crawl, no Claude call. The fact goes into the open draft or a new draft made from the approved version (approved facts copied and still approved), sourced to the member who typed it (`approved_facts.added_by`); approving the draft asks only about the new facts.
- Follow-ups: Daniekeys' location fact ("Based in Ilorin, Kwara State, serves clients across Nigeria") gives a reachable tech segment with Ilorin where it fits; the soap test is re-run as "I make and sell liquid soap in Ilorin"; no non-bakery in the Ilorin bakery top 10.
- Mobile-first, with empty, loading and error states. Tests for the segment schema, the ranking rubric and workspace isolation.
